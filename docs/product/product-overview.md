# Product Overview

## Purpose

Nursing Platform serves two connected purposes: helping nurses prepare for nursing licensing examinations and helping healthcare employers discover qualified nurses for recruitment.

## Audiences

- **Nurses** build professional profiles, prepare for examinations, review their performance, and participate in recruitment contact workflows.
- **Healthcare employers** search for suitable nurse candidates and request recruitment contact.
- **Administrators** manage the product's users, reference information, examination content, and preparation-package content, offers, and promotions within their approved capabilities.

## Product domains

The approved product scope covers accounts and authentication, including account recovery; nurse and employer profiles; mock examinations; recruitment; commerce for examination access; preparation packages; and administration. A preparation package is commercially offered alongside standalone paid mock-exam access. Its four benefits are study materials, practice, one package-scoped exam attempt, and one analytical report.

The product is intended to support examination and recruitment across countries. A particular preparation package remains scoped to one examination's country and exam category; it is not a cross-country bundle.
